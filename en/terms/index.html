2. Purpose and audience

AnesthesiaSheet supports manually entered personal notes, times, vital signs, medication and event records, IN/OUT organization, and self-review. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, medication orders, dose selection, patient-specific recommendations, or automated clinical decisions. The app is intended for adult healthcare professionals or trainees acting with appropriate authorization and supervision.

Facility-specific dilution concentrations are optional, user-confirmed record settings for manual transcription into a medication log. A user may choose not to use facility settings for a case. The values are not recommended concentrations, remain hidden from the intraoperative screen until confirmed by the user, and are not used to calculate a dose or infusion rate.

Time records are based on the device clock or manual edits. Editing a saved time requires an explicit save action, and corrections or removals may appear in a supplementary change history.
