Case notes stayon device by default.

The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Case data is not sent to a developer-operated server, and sharing or export happens only through user action.

  • On-device storage
  • No account required
  • No advertising SDK
  • No analytics SDK

Medical limitations

Anesthesia Memo is not an official anesthesia record, medical device, diagnostic tool, or medication order system. It does not diagnose, treat, select doses, provide patient-specific recommendations, issue alarms, or serve as emergency communication.

  • Not a substitute for official records
  • Not for use as a medical device
  • Not a basis for diagnosis or treatment decisions
  • Do not enter patient-identifying information
