How it works

  1. Case Info

    Enter a case summary and timestamps without information that identifies the patient.

  2. Intraop Records

    Enter vitals, medications, events, and IN/OUT, then view them on a five-minute chart and trend graph.

  3. Review

    Return to completed cases, postoperative follow-up notes, and searchable archives.

  4. Export and backup

    Single-case export is free. Bulk export and encrypted backups require Pro.

Medical limitations

Anesthesia Memo is not an official anesthesia record, medical device, diagnostic tool, or medication order system. It does not diagnose, treat, select doses, provide patient-specific recommendations, issue alarms, or serve as emergency communication.

  • Not a substitute for official records
  • Not for use as a medical device
  • Not a basis for diagnosis or treatment decisions
  • Do not enter patient-identifying information
